Our aim

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

What we have done

  • Everything works with a keyboard, with a visible focus outline and a "Skip to main content" link.
  • Pages use headings and labelled form fields, and errors are announced to screen readers.
  • Text meets AA contrast, and animation is reduced if your device asks for it.
  • Pages work on phones, tablets and desktops and can be zoomed to 200%.

Known limits

The staff harbour map is a visual aid; the same information is available as lists in the side panel.
