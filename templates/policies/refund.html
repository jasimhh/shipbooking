Where refunds go

Refunds are sent through Razorpay to the card, UPI account or bank you paid with. We cannot refund to a different account.

How much

The amount comes from the Cancellation Policy. A refund can never be more than you paid.

How long

Refund timelines: to be confirmed with the payment provider and operators.

The booking page shows each refund's status (pending, processing, succeeded or failed). If a refund fails, our team retries it and contacts you.

Duplicate or wrong charges

If you were charged twice or charged for a booking that was not confirmed, contact support with your booking reference. We check every payment against Razorpay's records.
