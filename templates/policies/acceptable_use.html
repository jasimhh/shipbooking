Please don't

  • Make bookings in someone else's name without their permission, or use stolen payment details.
  • Try to get into accounts, staff areas or data that aren't yours.
  • Overload the site, scrape it automatically or get around rate limits.
  • Send abusive, threatening or unlawful messages to staff or other passengers.
  • Resell tickets where the operator does not allow it.

What we may do

Block requests, cancel fraudulent bookings, suspend accounts and, where required, report to the authorities.
