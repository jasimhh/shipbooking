Cancelling a confirmed booking

Open the booking under Account → Bookings and choose "Request cancellation", with a reason. You can do this until departure. Reservations that are still waiting for payment can be cancelled straight away and nothing is charged.

If the operator cancels

Bookings on a sailing cancelled by the operator are cancelled for you and refunded in full. You are notified by email.

Process

You will see the status of your request on the booking page and by email. Refunds go back to the original payment method; see the Refund Policy.
