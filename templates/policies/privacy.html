What we collect

  • Account: email address, password (stored only as a secure hash), optional name and phone number.
  • Bookings: contact email and phone for the trip, and for each passenger their name, age and gender (gender is optional and can be "prefer not to say").
  • Payments: amounts, status and the payment reference from Razorpay. We never see or store card numbers, CVV, UPI PINs or bank passwords; you enter those on Razorpay's checkout.
  • Support: messages you send us and our replies.
  • Technical: IP address on security-sensitive actions (kept in the audit log), and the cookies described in the Cookie Policy.

Why we use it

  • To make and manage your booking, issue tickets and let crew check passengers on board.
  • To take payment and send refunds.
  • To tell you about your booking, schedule changes, cancellations and refunds.
  • To answer support requests and complaints.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent fraud.

Who we share it with

  • The ferry operator running your sailing gets the passenger list for that sailing only.
  • Razorpay processes your payment.
  • Our email provider delivers booking emails.
  • Authorities, where the law requires it.

We do not sell personal data.

How long we keep it

See the Data Retention Policy.

Your rights

In the Privacy centre you can download your data, ask us to correct or delete it, withdraw consent, or raise a grievance.

Children

Accounts are for adults. A child's details are only collected as a passenger on a booking made by an adult.

Security

Passwords are hashed, pages are served over HTTPS in production, staff access is limited by role, and access to passenger lists is logged.
